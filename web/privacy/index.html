Privacy policy

Cortex is a private operations tool run by Rashad Al-Safar for his own use. It is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.

Google account data

When the owner connects his own Google account, Cortex reads and writes his calendar, email and files only to carry out tasks he asks for, such as checking availability, booking events and filing documents. Nothing is sent or published without his approval.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and the data Cortex works with are stored on a private server controlled by the owner. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings, after which Cortex can no longer read or change anything in that account.
